Press a box in a stage's diagram and its knobs open under the drawing.

![The Growler voice card. Sync and FM wires join the oscillators, and noise and feedback enter at the mixer.](/bender/blog/voice-card-diagram.webp)

A box lights when a knob inside it is off stock. These drums have been rolled,
and PADS is the open box.

![The toy drums. Pads and pattern feed the triggers, which feed the one-shots, tanks, metal bank and noise, then the amps and the DAC.](/bender/blog/drum-voices-diagram.webp)

The FM chip draws its operators as a close-up under the chip's box.

![The FM chip's operators. The modulator has feedback, and the carrier and LFO sit beside it.](/bender/blog/fm-operators-zoom.webp)

Each bus is a ribbon with a trace per bit. Press a trace to put the knife on it.

![The toy keyboard's buses. Song select and counter into the ROM, the ROM into the note table, the note table into the divider.](/bender/blog/bus-ribbons.webp)

The tape machine's wires light once its mix is up.

![The tape machine. The board and dry wire, noise reduction in, record head, tape loop, replay head, noise reduction out.](/bender/blog/tape-diagram.webp)
