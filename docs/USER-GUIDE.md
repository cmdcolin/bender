# User guide

## Play

![Plinky: the deck with REC, HOLD and the octave switches above three octaves of keys, letters printed on the sixteen keys under a typing hand.](img/guide-keyboard.jpg)

- Keys, letters or [MIDI](MIDI.md)
- Record a melody onto a piano roll
- Arpeggiator, auto bass-chord, key lock

## Drum machine

![The toy drum machine's pad deck: eight numbered rubber pads, kick through cymbal, under the play and REC switches and a small copy of the drum pattern.](img/guide-drums.jpg)

- Eight voices, sixteen steps, polymeter, dice steps
- Pads, tap tempo, record hits live
- A sampled kit made from itself
- Dozens of [bends](BENDS.md)

## More instruments

![Glint's keybed: a green circuit board with TOY GATE and HOLD switches, an octave switch, and rows of push-button keys.](img/guide-fm.jpg)

- **Glint:** FM chip
- **Parlor:** eight-bit sampler
- **Growler:** ladder-filter monosynth
- **Four-track:** tape loops, no-input mixer, feedback squeal

## The trigger patch

![The trigger patch diagram: Clatter, Plinky and Glint as three boxes, with the wires kit → keys, keys → kit, gate and kit → FM between them.](img/guide-trigger.jpg)

- Drum hits play the keys, and keys fire the drums

## Bend

- Letter keys become sixteen dealt bends
- Six throws: spin, dive, brake, crash, drop, crush

![The patch bay: three of four LFO wires patched (LFO to toy level, envelope to FM level, drum hit to comb pitch), each with a depth slider and a + mod button.](img/guide-patchbay.jpg)

- **+ mod** wires an LFO to any control

## Presets

- Click to load, drag to morph
- Random rolls, **wreck it**, **dub**
- **Drift** wanders on its own

## Record

- Wav export with stems
- Tape-stop varispeed

## Phone

- [`/pocket/`](https://cmdcolin.github.io/bender/pocket/): swipe, drag and tilt
  to bend

## Share

- The link is the whole board
- Sign in to save named voices
