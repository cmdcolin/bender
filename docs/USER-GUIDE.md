# User guide

Open the app and press keys. Type the letters printed on them, or click.

![The whole app: keyboards, a drum box and pedals on the bench, the header and the signal path map.](../public/guide/guide-app.jpg)

![The header: random, mutate, drift, arc, reset, save, presets, share.](../public/guide/guide-header.jpg)

**random** rolls a board, **presets** lists the named ones, **share** copies a
link holding the whole board. **Space** runs the drums.

[![The signal path map: power supply, the machines, mix bus, a pedal and output.](../public/guide/guide-map.jpg)](https://cmdcolin.github.io/bender/app/#p=UHgzZKQCeA&v=3 'open this in the app')

Click a box on the map to open its knobs. **+ add a stage** puts more on the
board.

## Plinky

![Plinky: a toy keyboard with effect buttons over its keys.](../public/guide/guide-keyboard.jpg)

![The toy chip's die: keyboard, key matrix, voices, tune ROM, bus, envelope, output pin and clock.](../public/guide/guide-die.jpg)

A toy keyboard chip on a battery. Press a box on the die to open that part.

## Clatter

![Clatter: a toy drum box with pads and a pattern strip.](../public/guide/guide-drums.jpg)

![The drum box's voices: triggers, one-shots, tanks, metal, noise, amps and a sample ROM.](../public/guide/guide-drum-voices.jpg)

Tap pads, draw steps, press ▶. Clicking a step cycles it: hit, dice, ghost (a
soft hit), off. **Wander** lets the beat stray by itself.

## Bend it

![Bend keys: letter keys, each switching on a bend.](../public/guide/guide-bend-keys.jpg)

**bend keys** puts a bend on each letter. Hold one down.

![Two pedals, Ricochet and Boing, joined by a cord.](../public/guide/guide-pedals.jpg)

**add pedal** adds effects. Drag a cord's jack to reorder them.

[![The power supply: cells, reset chip and clip feeding the machines.](../public/guide/guide-power.jpg)](https://cmdcolin.github.io/bender/app/#p=rQUCAAI&v=3 'open this in the app')

Turn **Starve** up while a chord rings.

## Patch it

[![The trigger patch: cables between Clatter, Plinky, Glint, Parlor and Growler.](../public/guide/guide-trigger.jpg)](https://cmdcolin.github.io/bender/app/#p=UGQHAnECAAIAAg&v=3 'open this in the app')

Amber cables carry notes, purple cables carry drum hits.

[![The patch bay: wires, each from a source to a knob with a depth slider.](../public/guide/guide-patchbay.jpg)](https://cmdcolin.github.io/bender/app/#p=UGRCUCgCADYBBgA6ARI&v=3 'open this in the app')

**+ mod** beside any knob wires an LFO to it.

## The arc

[![The arc's controls: Crazy, the arc's shape, its length in bars, glide or jump, and a wild roll.](../public/guide/guide-arc.jpg)](https://cmdcolin.github.io/bender/app/#p=1AaMAQ&v=3 'open this in the app')

**arc** swings the board toward a wild twin of itself and back, in time with the
kit.
